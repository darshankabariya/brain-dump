Everything made while studying the zk-inference-prover

16 pages, animations, videos and source documents, in the order to read them. Each card opens the local file; the published page is the claude.ai copy, which is private unless shared. Built from index.json by tools/build_index.py.

Where to start

  1. Read the Berrang Research Primer, sections 1 to 3, for the problem and the cost.
  2. Step through the Llama Decode Walkthrough, then Decode and Prove for one real prompt.
  3. Take the proof side in order: Polynomial Agreement Bound, The One-Cell Amplifier, Tape to Polynomial, One Proof, Step by Step.
  4. Then the prover end to end: ZK Inference Prover Flow while reading the code, Prover Compute Phases for where the time goes.
  5. For a listen or a watch: the two NotebookLM sources and the video.

The One-Cell Amplifier

Change one cell of the tape and the curve through the cells changes almost everywhere: the agreement bound put to work, which is why a checker reading a few random points catches a single wrong cell. Flip cells yourself.
